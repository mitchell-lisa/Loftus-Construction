# Color

The palette is the logo, plus the warm neutrals of the work. Hi-vis orange shows up in the Brownsville vests (about `#d6901c`). It does not earn a place. It is not in the mark, and an orange accent is what a generic contractor template reaches for.

| Role | Hex | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#0014e0` | The wordmark blue. Speed lines, links, and the bid button. |
| Deep | `#222e61` | The dimensional sign. Headlines, the hero plate, the award, the preview bar, the phone bar. |
| Paper | `#f3f0e8` | Page ground. Warm, off the concrete decks, so it is not a blank white template. |
| White | `#ffffff` | Header and footer only, so the logo sits on a clean field. |
| Ink | `#1c1e22` | Body text. |
| Muted | `#4e545c` | Secondary text. |
| Rule | `#cfc8bc` | Hairlines in the record and the spec lists. The speed lines stay primary blue, or white on deep. |

Primary blue is not set as text on deep. Both are dark, and the pair fails. On the deep plate, type is white.

## Contrast

WCAG AA is 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text. These are the pairs in use.

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Ink `#1c1e22` on paper `#f3f0e8` | 14.7 to 1 |
| Ink `#1c1e22` on white `#ffffff` | 16.7 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on paper `#f3f0e8` | 6.7 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on white `#ffffff` | 7.6 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on paper `#f3f0e8` | 11.3 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on white `#ffffff` | 12.9 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on white `#ffffff` | 9.6 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on paper `#f3f0e8` | 8.5 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on deep `#222e61` | 12.9 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on primary `#0014e0` | 9.6 to 1 |
