# Color

Corporate and cool. Content sits on white and a light cool gray. The header, footer, and phone bar are a near-black navy. Sign navy and the logo blue stay on the content.

| Role | Hex | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Chrome | `#12162a` | Header, footer, and the sticky phone bar. Near-black navy. |
| Chrome muted | `#c5cad3` | Secondary type on chrome: inactive nav, address, footer disclosure. |
| White | `#ffffff` | Page ground. Type and speed lines on chrome and on the sign navy. |
| Cool gray | `#f2f4f6` | Alternating content bands. |
| Deep | `#222e61` | The dimensional sign. Page plates, headlines, the award band, the preview bar. |
| Primary | `#0014e0` | The wordmark blue. Speed lines and links on white and cool gray. The bid button. |
| Ink | `#1c1e22` | Body text on white and cool gray. |
| Muted | `#4e545c` | Secondary text on white and cool gray. |
| Plate muted | `#d5d8e2` | Secondary text on the deep plate. |
| Rule | `#d5d8de` | Cool gray hairlines in the record and the spec lists. |

Primary blue is not set as text or as a line on chrome or on deep. Both pairs fail. On chrome and on the deep plate, type and speed lines are white. The header wordmark is the current logo with its alpha recolored white. The footer mark is the dimensional sign recolored white.

## Contrast

WCAG AA is 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text. These are the pairs in use.

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Ink `#1c1e22` on white `#ffffff` | 16.7 to 1 |
| Ink `#1c1e22` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 15.1 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on white `#ffffff` | 7.6 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 6.9 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on white `#ffffff` | 12.9 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 11.7 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on white `#ffffff` | 9.6 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 8.8 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on deep `#222e61` | 12.9 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on primary `#0014e0` | 9.6 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on chrome `#12162a` | 17.9 to 1 |
| Chrome muted `#c5cad3` on chrome `#12162a` | 10.9 to 1 |
| Plate muted `#d5d8e2` on deep `#222e61` | 9.0 to 1 |

Primary `#0014e0` on chrome `#12162a` is 1.9 to 1 and is not used.
