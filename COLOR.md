# Color

One navy and white. Logo blue is a thin accent on white only.

| Role | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#222e61` | Header, footer, sticky phone bar, preview banner, hero plate, every page-header plate, the award panel. Headlines on white. |
| White | `#ffffff` | Every content section. Type on navy. Speed lines on navy. The bid button on navy, with navy text. |
| Logo blue | `#0014e0` | Speed lines on white, link underlines on white, and the bid button on white. |
| Ink | `#1c1e22` | Body text on white. |
| Muted | `#4e545c` | Secondary text on white. |
| Mist | `#c5cad3` | Secondary text on navy: inactive navigation, address, footer disclosure. |
| Rule | `#d5d8de` | Hairlines between rows and at the footer disclosure. |

There is no second navy and no gray section band. Speed lines on navy are white. Speed lines on white are logo blue.

## Contrast

WCAG AA is 4.5 to 1 for normal text.

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Ink `#1c1e22` on white `#ffffff` | 16.7 to 1 |
| Navy `#222e61` on white `#ffffff` | 12.9 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on white `#ffffff` | 7.6 to 1 |
| Logo blue `#0014e0` on white `#ffffff` | 9.6 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on navy `#222e61` | 12.9 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on logo blue `#0014e0` | 9.6 to 1 |
| Navy `#222e61` on white `#ffffff`, the bid button on navy | 12.9 to 1 |
| Mist `#c5cad3` on navy `#222e61` | 7.8 to 1 |
