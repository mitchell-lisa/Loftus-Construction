# Color

Corporate and cool. White field, light cool gray bands, navy and the logo blue used with restraint. No warm paper, beige rules, or jobsite orange.

| Role | Hex | Why |
|---|---|---|
| White | `#ffffff` | Page ground, header, and footer. |
| Cool gray | `#f2f4f6` | Alternating section bands. |
| Deep | `#222e61` | The dimensional sign. Headlines, the hero plate, the award, the preview bar, the phone bar. |
| Primary | `#0014e0` | The wordmark blue. Speed lines, links, and the bid button. |
| Ink | `#1c1e22` | Body text. Crisp dark gray. |
| Muted | `#4e545c` | Secondary text. |
| Rule | `#d5d8de` | Cool gray hairlines in the record and the spec lists. Speed lines stay primary blue, or white on deep. |

Primary blue is not set as text on deep. Both are dark, and the pair fails. On the deep plate, type is white.

## Contrast

WCAG AA is 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text. These are the pairs in use.

| Pair | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Ink `#1c1e22` on white `#ffffff` | 16.7 to 1 |
| Ink `#1c1e22` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 15.1 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on white `#ffffff` | 7.6 to 1 |
| Muted `#4e545c` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 6.9 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on white `#ffffff` | 12.9 to 1 |
| Deep `#222e61` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 11.7 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on white `#ffffff` | 9.6 to 1 |
| Primary `#0014e0` on cool gray `#f2f4f6` | 8.8 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on deep `#222e61` | 12.9 to 1 |
| White `#ffffff` on primary `#0014e0` | 9.6 to 1 |
